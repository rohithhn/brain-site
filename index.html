How I work

  1. 01

    Capture

    I read your Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Gemini CLI and Cursor sessions as they happen, into one local SQLite store on your machine.

  2. 02

    Distill

    While you sleep, brain dream turns what I heard into a wiki I keep rewriting. When you finally fix something hard, I draft the lesson for you to approve.

  3. 03

    Rank

    I rank by relevance, then by proof: how often a memory was used, and whether you said it helped. What misleads you sinks.

  4. 04

    Inject

    Before you type, I write a ~2,000-token briefing into CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md and GEMINI.md, inside my own markers. I never touch your text.

  5. 05

    Measure

    brain eval quizzes me on your own history. If a change makes me remember worse, it doesn't ship.

Every session start

A briefing, not a dump.

With 10,000 memories, pasting everything would cost 1.13 million tokens. The context I hand over is about 1,300: 99.88% smaller. The start-up briefing is capped at 2,000, so tools that cut off long start-up messages never drop the end.

Who I belong to

Sealed with your key. Kept in your repo.

I travel as one age-encrypted blob, a Release asset on your private repo. My key lives only in your Keychain. A leaked repo or a leaked token exposes nothing readable.

Why a Release asset and not git? The blob is replaced on every sync. Release assets are replaced in place and don't touch the LFS quota.

A few more things about me

A control panel on localhost.

Search memory, browse the wiki, approve learnings, rate what got injected, and watch capture freshness and sync age.

Bring your history with you.

Switching from another memory tool? One command imports your whole history, read-only and verified, wires every tool, and can be undone.

Skills that use the memory.

Shipped skills that pull context before work and write decisions back after it.

brain-recallcapture-decisiondebug-systematically onboard-codebasereview-changeswrite-tests

Many machines, one brain.

brain sync on one laptop, brain restore on the next. brain merge reconciles memory from more than one.

It never eats your files.

Every write path is idempotent. Injection lives inside BRAIN:START/END markers, and MCP wiring is additive: it never touches entries it didn't create.

Secrets scrubbed before storage.

Settings are redacted before commit, and memory, wiki and learnings are encrypted. Skills and agents stay plaintext so they stay portable.

What I can't do yet

Stated plainly.

  • macOS first. Keychain and launchd are the blockers; neither is fundamental.
  • Whole-blob sync. Fine at tens of megabytes, not elegant.
  • Merge-based multi-machine. No live conflict resolution.
  • Two tools are pull-only forever. Claude Desktop and ChatGPT desktop, for architectural reasons.
  • Cursor push is per-repo. A new Cursor project starts without the digest until you wire it.
